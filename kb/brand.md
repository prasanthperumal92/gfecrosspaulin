# Brand and design guidelines

## Colours (sampled from the Crosspaulin logo, S1)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | `#234997` | Primary: headings, bands, table headers ("CROSS" in the logo) |
| Blue dark | `#162F66` | Stripes, heavy-duty chip |
| Orange | `#DE8225` | Accent: kickers, highlights ("PAULIN" in the logo) |
| Red | `#E31D24` | Logo diamond and "PANKAJ". Use sparingly. |
| Packing-list red | `#D42427` | Used in `docs/packinglist.html` |

## Typography
Montserrat 600–800 for headings and Inter 400–700 for body text. Both are embedded locally from `brochure/assets/fonts/` (fontsource).

## Logos
- Crosspaulin: `brochure/assets/crosspaulin-logo.png` (transparent, cropped from the old brochure at 300 dpi). Place it on white. Never redraw it.
- Grace Ferro Exports: `brochure/assets/gfe-logo.png` (373 × 113, from the website). It reads "GRACE FERRO EXPORTS PVT LTD · Trustful Exporters". Only a small raster is available, so ask the owner for a vector.

## Visual motifs
Diagonal blue stripes (from the old brochure header) and white horse mascots (in the old brochure only; not reused).
The printed sheets carry a triangle label with the brand, GSM and a Hindi tagline.

## Writing rules for export material
International English, metric units first, no invented numbers, manufacturer claims labelled as such, `[TBC]` for gaps.
