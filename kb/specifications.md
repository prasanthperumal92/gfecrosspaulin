## Range beyond the four standard grades
- GSM available: 70, 90, 120, 150, 200, 250 (S5).
- Colours: blue, silver, white, green, custom (S5). Yellow sheets (90 and 150 GSM) appear in the photos (S10) but are not listed on the website; confirm before advertising yellow.
- Formats: 54 standard cut sizes ([sizes-and-packing.md](sizes-and-packing.md)), rolls, or custom cut.
- Roll example: 200 GSM, 100 m × 6 ft, about 36.5 kg per roll (S5).

## Certification
- The 120 GSM photo shows an ISI-style mark (S10). Confirm the IS standard and licence number before claiming BIS certification; the likely standard is IS 14611 ([standards.md](standards.md)).
- Test reports are "available depending on order and destination" (S5).
