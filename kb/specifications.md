## Material
- Product name: cross-laminated multi-layer tarpaulin; "A product of PANKAJ" (S12).
- Layers: minimum 2, maximum 4. Construction "as agreed between the purchaser and supplier" (S12).
- Polymer: combination of LDPE, LLDPE and plastomer, 100% virgin (S12). **This replaces the website's "HDPE + LDPE" (S5)**; the website should be corrected.
- UV-stabilised (S12).

## Range
- GSM: 70, 90, 120, 150, 200, 250 (S5, S12).
- Colours: blue, yellow, red, olive green, black & silver, black, natural, milky white (S12). Custom colours on request (S12). The website's list (blue, silver, white, green) is older (S5).
- Maximum width 2,750 mm; maximum roll length 1,000 m (S12).
- Formats: 54 standard cut sizes ([sizes-and-packing.md](sizes-and-packing.md)), rolls, or custom cut. Roll example: 200 GSM, 100 m × 6 ft, about 36.5 kg per roll (S5).
