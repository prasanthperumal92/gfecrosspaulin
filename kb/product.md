# Crosspaulin™ cross-laminated multilayer tarpaulin

## What it is
A tarpaulin made by bonding oriented polyethylene film layers at crossed angles into one sheet. An ordinary tarpaulin is woven from flat HDPE tapes and then coated. Cross-lamination spreads strength in more than one direction and leaves no weave to fray or leak. The surface has a fine crossed texture (photo: `images/crosspaulin_cross_braid.jpeg`).

- Raw material: 100% virgin LDPE, LLDPE and plastomer, UV-stabilised, in 2 to 4 layers (S12). The website's "HDPE + LDPE" (S5) is outdated.
- BIS-licensed under IS 14611:2024; made in an ISO 9001:2015 certified plant (S12, S13).
- The website also calls it a "multi-layer cross-laminated plastic film" (S5).
- Sheets are printed with the GSM, the brand mark and a Hindi tagline (S10).
