# Export terms (all from S5 unless noted)

| Term | Detail |
|---|---|
| Lead time | About 35–40 days of production, excluding ocean transit |
| Incoterms | Generally FOB Mumbai; CIF quoted on request, by destination port and freight |
| Ports of loading | Chennai, Mumbai, Nhava Sheva; other Indian ports on request |
| Payment | LC (usance / UPAS) · 50% advance + 50% against scanned B/L · 30% advance + 70% D/P |
| Packaging | Bulk, retail-ready, palletised, shrink-wrapped, custom export packing |
| Container loading (200 GSM rolls, 100 m × 6 ft, ~36.5 kg) | 20 ft: about 300–320 rolls / 11.5–12 MT · 40 ft: about 600–640 rolls / 23–24 MT. Varies with GSM, size and packing |
| Samples | Available; cost refundable against qualifying bulk orders; buyer pays courier |
| Documentation | Test reports, applicable certifications and export documents, depending on order and destination |
| Price | On request |
| MOQ | **[TBC]**, not stated anywhere |
