# Manufacturer: PANKAJ®

- Crosspaulin™ is branded "A product of PANKAJ®" on its logo and packaging (S1, S10).
- Brochure wording: "Crosspaulin™ by PANKAJ®". Do not name a legal entity until the owner confirms it.

## Unverified lead (do not publish)
Web listings tie the **Pankaj** tarpaulin brand to **Malsons Polymers Limited**, Kolkata, West Bengal (GST 19AABCM9941J1ZZ). They make LLDPE film, multilayer tarpaulin, shade net and mulching film, and one listing claims ISO 9001:2008 and 20,000 TPA capacity (S8, S9). Founding years conflict across listings (1995 / 2004 / 2018).
None of these listings mention Crosspaulin by name, so confirm with the owner.

## Trademark rules
- Keep ™ on Crosspaulin and ® on PANKAJ.
- Use the logo crop `brochure/assets/crosspaulin-logo.png` only. Do not redraw or recolour it.
- Legal line used in the brochure: "Crosspaulin™ and PANKAJ® are trademarks of their respective owner. Grace Ferro Exports Pvt Ltd is a distributor and exporter, not the manufacturer."
