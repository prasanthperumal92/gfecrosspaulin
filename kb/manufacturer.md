## Disclosure rule (owner decision, 2026-10-19)
**Do not name Malsons Polymers, its address, its BIS licence number or its ISO certificate number in buyer-facing material.**
Use "Crosspaulin™ by PANKAJ®", "BIS-licensed under IS 14611:2024" and "made in an ISO 9001:2015 certified plant". Give the numbers on request.
Commercial terms between Malsons and Grace Ferro are internal ([export-terms.md](export-terms.md)).

## Trademark rules
- Keep ™ on Crosspaulin and ® on PANKAJ.
- Use the logo crop `brochure/assets/crosspaulin-logo.png` only. Do not redraw or recolour it.
- Legal line used in the brochure: "Crosspaulin™ and PANKAJ® are trademarks of their respective owner. Grace Ferro Exports Pvt Ltd is a distributor and exporter, not the manufacturer."
