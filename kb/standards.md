## IS 14611, multilayered cross-laminated tarpaulins
Current revision: **IS 14611:2024** (S12). The title below comes from the 2016 revision (S7): "Multilayered cross laminated sheets/tarpaulins/covers/agricultural films – Specification".
Related test standards cited by the manufacturer: IS 7016 (Part 7) for waterproofing, and IS 7016 (Part 8) for accelerated ageing (S12).

### Duty classes (by GSM, per the 2016 revision; check against 2024)
| Class | GSM band |
|---|---|
| LN, light duty | 60–100 |
| MN, medium duty | 110–150 |
| HN, heavy duty | 200–250 |

### Minimum requirements (secondary source S6; verify against the BIS text)
| Property | Longitudinal | Transverse |
|---|---|---|
| Tensile strength | 250 kg/cm² | 200 kg/cm² |
| Elongation at break | 400% | 300% |
| Tear strength (LN / MN / HN, garbled in source) | ~1200 / 2080 / 3000 | ~900 / 1550 / 2750 |
| Dart impact (unlabelled) | 250 g / 400 g / 700 g | |
| After ageing 168 h at 70 °C | ≤30% decrease in tensile/tear | |
| After UV weathering | ≥40% of original strength retained | |

The manufacturer's declared tensile and elongation (S12) match these minimums. Its UV retention (65–68%) and post-ageing tensile (232/202 kg/cm²) meet them.
Do not publish the S6 tear and dart values; the brochure says "as per IS 14611:2024 for the grade".
