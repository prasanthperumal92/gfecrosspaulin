# Purity PVC tarpaulin

A double-side PVC-coated tarpaulin, sold as rolls or ready-made sheets. Grace Ferro is the distributor and exporter, not the manufacturer. Sources: [`purity/sources.md`](../purity/sources.md) (P1–P4, G1).

## Known (P1)
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Coating | PVC, both sides |
| Grades | 400, 430, 550, 650, 760 GSM |
| Roll | 1.5 m × 50 m = 75 m² (807 sq ft) |
| Sheets | 15×12, 18×15, 24×18, 30×18 ft (180, 270, 432, 540 sq ft) |

Derived weights (GSM × area, nominal; computed in `purity/tools/build.py`): roll 30.0 / 32.3 / 41.3 / 48.8 / 57.0 kg.

## From Grace Ferro's PVC page (G1, generic for the whole PVC line)
Temperature "generally rated from -30°C to +70°C". The page also gives 300–1300+ GSM and 0.3–1.2 mm thickness for the whole line; these are not Purity-specific and are not used.
