Goal

Create a 6-page A4 PDF export brochure for Purity PVC Tarpaulin, made from HTML/CSS, using the same pipeline as the Crosspaulin brochure. It is for overseas buyers. It covers what the product is, its features, the grade range, roll and sheet forms, applications, and how to order.

  • Grace Ferro Exports Pvt Ltd publishes the brochure as distributor and exporter. It is not the manufacturer. Company facts come from kb/company.md.
  • Purity is the product brand. The brand owner, manufacturer, trademark status (™ or ®) and logo are unknown. See the open questions.
  • The product is a double-side PVC-coated tarpaulin in five grades (400–760 GSM), sold as 1.5 m × 50 m rolls or as ready-made sheets in four sizes.
  • Write in international English, with metric units first and imperial in brackets.
The price list is internal. todo/Screenshot_2026-10-29_21-22-23.png is a domestic INR price list (freight and 18% GST included). Use it only for the GSM range, roll dimensions and sheet sizes. No price, rate, GST figure or ₹ sign may appear in the brochure, the website or any file published from _site/.

Unknown specifications (research, then ask the owner)

Search for a Purity datasheet first. If none is found, the owner must get one from the manufacturer. Until then each value is [TBC] in kb/, and the brochure leaves the row out rather than printing [TBC].

  • Base fabric: polyester or nylon, woven or knitted scrim, yarn denier, thread count
  • Thickness per grade (mm)
  • Tensile strength warp/weft, tear strength, coating adhesion, with the test method (ISO 1421, DIN 53354, ISO 2411 or similar)
  • Temperature range (the features list says "freezing cold through peak summer heat"; we need numbers)
  • UV stabilisation: additive only, or a tested rating (hours, method)
  • Fire retardant option: which grades, which standard (DIN 4102 B1, NFPA 701, EN 13501-1, IS 15061 or other), and whether there is a test report
  • Colours per grade; finish (gloss or matt); lacquered or not
  • Fabrication: eyelet material (photos show brass) and spacing, hem type (welded or stitched), rope or reinforced corners
  • Certifications: BIS/ISI (IS 7903 covers PVC-coated tarpaulins; confirm whether it applies), ISO 9001 of the plant, REACH/RoHS for EU buyers
  • Packing: roll and sheet packing, pieces per bale, container loading per grade
  • HS code (probably 5907 or 6306 12/19 for made-up sheets; confirm with the customs broker)

Copy rules

  • Rewrite the features in our own words. Remove absolute claims: "colour and tensile strength do not change" becomes "resists UV fading and loss of strength"; "does not tear" becomes "strong and tear-resistant".
  • "Optional Fire Hydrant" in the source is a typo for "fire retardant". Write "Fire-retardant (FR) version on request". Do not say "meets industry norms" until a standard and report are supplied.
  • Do not copy any export terms from Crosspaulin (24 MT MOQ, 35–40 days, ports, payment, HS code). They are product-specific. Use only the general GFE terms the owner confirms for Purity.
  • Do not invent specs, certifications, MOQ, prices or lead times. Every number in the brochure is either in P1, derived from P1 in build.py, or sourced in purity/sources.md.
