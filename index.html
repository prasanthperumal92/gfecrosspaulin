The manufacturer

Crosspaulin™ by PANKAJ®

Crosspaulin is a multilayer cross-laminated tarpaulin made by PANKAJ®. It is built for people who need a cover that lasts more than one season.

It is made from 100% virgin LDPE, LLDPE and plastomer in two to four cross-laminated layers, with UV stabilisers. It is BIS-licensed under IS 14611:2024 and made in an ISO 9001:2015 certified plant. It comes in several weights, sizes and colours, with custom specifications and private labelling for bulk orders.

The technology

What cross-lamination does

An ordinary tarpaulin is woven from flat plastic tapes and then coated. Crosspaulin is made differently: oriented film layers are bonded at crossed angles into one sheet. Strength runs in more than one direction, and there is no weave to open up, fray or let water through.
