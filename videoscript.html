Crosspaulin — Product Video Script

GraceFerro Exports · 2-minute product film · Draft 1

Runtime2:00 (8 scenes)
Format16:9 master, 9:16 cut-down for reels
Voice-over~270 words, calm and confident, English (Hindi/Tamil dub optional)
MusicWarm acoustic → driving percussion build from Scene 4
Check before the shoot: sizes (6 × 4 ft to 24 × 15 ft), weights (70–300 GSM), colour choice, custom sizes and fixed pack counts come from our product catalogue. The product is a cross-laminated tarpaulin. The claims about UV stabilisation, aluminium eyelets and reinforced edges are typical for this product but are not recorded in our catalogue — confirm each with production and drop any we can't stand behind.
01

Cold open — the storm

0:00 – 0:10
Visuals
  • Dark clouds rolling over a farm field, wind bending crops.
  • Fast cuts: first raindrops hitting dust, a farmer looking up, harvested grain sacks in the open.
  • A blue Crosspaulin snaps open in the wind and settles over the sacks.
Voice-over

“The weather doesn't wait. A season of work can be lost in one afternoon.”

SFX: thunder, wind, rain onsetOn-screen: none
02

Brand reveal

0:10 – 0:18
Visuals
  • Rain drumming on the stretched sheet; beads of water roll off.
  • Slow push-in, cut to black.
  • GraceFerro Exports logo, then the product name.
Voice-over

“That's why we make Crosspaulin.”

On-screen: CROSSPAULIN — by GraceFerro ExportsMusic: sting on logo
03

Inside the factory — how it's made

0:18 – 0:38
Visuals
  • Rolls of film feeding into the lamination line, one running lengthwise, one crosswise.
  • Graphic overlay: the layers rotate into a criss-cross and fuse into one sheet.
  • Macro shot of the finished surface catching the light; a hand flexes it to show it's both strong and light.
  • Hands checking a sample on a GSM scale; QC inspector stamps a sheet.
  • Hem stitching, eyelets punched into the corners.
Voice-over

“Every Crosspaulin is cross-laminated: layers of film are bonded at right angles to each other, so the sheet is strong in every direction, stays light, and is waterproof right through. Each batch is weighed and inspected, then hemmed and fitted with eyelets — so it's ready to tie down the moment it leaves the bundle.”

On-screen: Cross-laminated · Strong in every direction · Weighed & checkedSFX: machine hum, punch press
04

Strength test

0:38 – 0:52
Visuals
  • Two workers pull a sheet taut from opposite ends — it holds.
  • Bucket of water poured onto a sagging sheet; underside stays dry (show the hand underneath).
  • Time-lapse: sheet in harsh midday sun, colour stays bright.
Voice-over

“It's built to take a pull, hold the water out, and stand up to long days under the sun.”

On-screen: Tear-resistant Waterproof Sun-readyMusic: percussion kicks in
05

Built for many jobs

0:52 – 1:12
Visuals (quick montage, ~3 s each)
  • Truck load being covered and roped at a highway dhaba.
  • Paddy drying on a Crosspaulin in a courtyard.
  • Construction site: sheet shading cement bags.
  • Fishing boat deck covered at the harbour.
  • Market stall roof in a light drizzle.
  • Relief-camp style shelter being pitched.
Voice-over

“On the highway, in the field, on the site, at the harbour, in the market — wherever something needs covering, Crosspaulin is doing the job.”

On-screen: one-word captions per shot — Transport · Farming · Construction · Fishing · Retail · Shelter
06

The range — sizes, weights, colours

1:12 – 1:32
Visuals
  • Warehouse wide shot, rows of folded bundles in different colours.
  • Motion graphic: a sheet grows from 6 × 4 ft to 24 × 15 ft.
  • A GSM slider sweeps 70 → 300 while the sheet on screen thickens.
  • Colour swatches flip past.
  • A worker cuts and hems a custom size to order.
Voice-over

“Pick the size you need — from six by four feet up to twenty-four by fifteen and beyond. Choose the weight, from a light seventy GSM to a heavy-duty three hundred, in the colour that suits your work. Need something different? We'll make it to your size.”

On-screen: 6×4 ft → 24×15 ft 70 – 300 GSM Custom sizes
07

Packed and shipped

1:32 – 1:48
Visuals
  • Sheets folded and stacked into labelled bundles (label shows size, GSM, pieces).
  • Bundles strapped onto a pallet, forklift loads a container.
  • Container doors close; truck pulls out of the GraceFerro gate.
Voice-over

“Every order is packed in fixed bundles, clearly labelled by size and weight — easy to count, easy to stock, and ready to ship across India and overseas.”

On-screen: Labelled bundles · Domestic & export
08

Close — back to the farm

1:48 – 2:00
Visuals
  • Storm has passed; sun breaks through.
  • Farmer pulls back the Crosspaulin — grain sacks are dry. A smile.
  • End card: logo, product name, contact details.
Voice-over

“Whatever the weather brings, cover it with confidence. Crosspaulin, from GraceFerro Exports.”

End card: GraceFerro Exports logo · CROSSPAULIN · email · phone · website · “Bulk & export enquiries welcome”
